Planning a Duck-Hunting Trip

Argentina is known for its varied landscapes and outdoor sporting opportunities. Before planning a trip, research your intended destination, applicable hunting regulations, travel requirements, and the services offered by your chosen outfitter.

Trip details can vary by destination, season, and package. Confirm current requirements and arrangements directly with the relevant operator before making plans.

Make informed plans

Use current, reliable information when comparing destinations. Verify travel documents, local rules, inclusions, and logistics before departure.

Choose with care

Choosing a Hunting Destination

Consider the experience you want, your travel arrangements, the season, and the details of each available package. Research can help you ask better questions before booking.

01

Research the area

Learn about the destination, expected travel route, local conditions, and location-specific requirements relevant to your visit.

02

Compare the package

Ask the operator to clarify what is included, what costs extra, the schedule, accommodation arrangements, and available support.

03

Confirm requirements

Check current hunting regulations, permits, transport rules, and travel documentation with official sources and the operator.

Before you depart

A Practical Preparation Checklist

Use this checklist as a starting point, then confirm the specifics for your destination and travel dates.

  • Confirm passport, entry, and travel-document requirements using official sources.
  • Ask about applicable hunting permits and regulations before booking.
  • Get written confirmation of package inclusions, dates, transport, and accommodation.
  • Check what equipment is supplied and what you are expected to bring.
  • Keep the operator's contact details and itinerary accessible while travelling.
